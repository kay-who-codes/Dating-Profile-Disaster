Your hand. Tap 3 cards to build your profile.

How to play

  1. Everyone opens this on their own phone and gets 8 cards, with at least one of each kind.
  2. Pick 3 cards. The order you tap is the order they read.
  3. Tap Show my profile and read it aloud, or hold up the screen.
  4. The group decides who wrote the worst profile and who would still get the most matches. Agree your own turns and scoring.
  5. Swap up to 3 cards once per hand, or take a new hand.
  6. Openers and blank wild cards are editable: type straight into them. Tap edit on a profile to change the wording.
